Wishio remembers the important days of the people close to you and helps you know what to give them.

NEVER MISS AN OCCASION
• Birthdays, name days and the gift-giving holidays of Moldova: March 8, June 1, September 1, Christmas, Easter.
• Name days are worked out from first names, following the church calendar. You just confirm them.
• Choose how many days ahead the reminder comes, at what time, and when not to be disturbed.
• Every Monday morning we email you the week's occasions.

KNOW WHAT TO GIVE
• Say what the person likes and what your budget is. Get products from shops in Moldova, with prices and a link to the shop.
• Not sure what they like? Describe them in a few words and we suggest matching interests.
• Save ideas, mark them when bought, and Wishio remembers what you gave, so you never repeat a gift.

FRIENDS TELL YOU THEMSELVES
• Send them your personal link: they add their own birthday and what they like, and it comes straight to you.
• On the same page you can publish your own wishlist, if you want.

START IN SECONDS
• Pick who to follow from your contacts. We only read names and birthdays.
• Or add people by hand: the app works fully without access to your contacts.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• We don't read phone numbers, emails or photos from your contacts.
• Notes about people are encrypted, and no one else sees your list.
• Download your data or delete your account right in the app.

In Romanian, Russian and English.
